## Upper Body Workouts

### Workout A: Chest & Triceps

#### Warm-Up (5 minutes)
- Arm circles (30 seconds each direction)
- Shoulder rolls (30 seconds)
- Push-up position hold (30 seconds)
- Light chest opener stretches

#### Main Workout

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Weight | Rest |
|----------|------|------|--------|------|
| **Push-Ups** (various grips) | 4 | 12-15 | Bodyweight | 60s |
| **Dumbbell Floor Press** | 4 | 10-12 | 8kg | 60s |
| **Incline Push-Ups** (feet elevated) | 3 | 12-15 | Bodyweight | 60s |
| **Dumbbell Flyes** (floor) | 3 | 12-15 | 5kg | 60s |
| **Diamond Push-Ups** | 3 | 8-12 | Bodyweight | 60s |
| **Overhead Tricep Extension** | 3 | 12-15 | 8kg | 45s |
| **Tricep Kickbacks** | 3 | 12-15 | 5kg | 45s |
| **Close-Grip Push-Ups** | 3 | To failure | Bodyweight | 60s |

#### Exercise Notes

**Push-Up Variations:**
- Standard: Hands shoulder-width apart
- Wide: Hands wider than shoulders (more chest)
- Close/Diamond: Hands together (more triceps)
- Decline: Feet elevated on chair/step (upper chest)

**Dumbbell Floor Press:**
- Lie on floor, dumbbells at chest level
- Press up, squeezing chest at top
- Floor limits range but protects shoulders

**Dumbbell Flyes:**
- Lie on floor, slight bend in elbows
- Lower weights out to sides slowly
- Squeeze chest to bring together

#### Main Workout

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Weight | Rest |
|----------|------|------|--------|------|
| **Bent-Over Rows** | 4 | 10-12 | 8kg each | 60s |
| **Single-Arm Rows** | 3 | 12 each | 8kg | 45s |
| **Reverse Flyes** | 3 | 15 | 5kg | 45s |
| **Superman Holds** | 3 | 30 seconds | Bodyweight | 45s |
| **Bicep Curls** (standing) | 4 | 12-15 | 8kg | 45s |
| **Hammer Curls** | 3 | 12-15 | 8kg | 45s |
| **Concentration Curls** | 3 | 12 each | 5kg | 45s |
| **Incline Curls** (lying on floor, arms behind) | 3 | 12-15 | 5kg | 45s |

#### Exercise Notes

**Bent-Over Rows:**
- Hinge at hips, back flat
- Pull elbows back, squeeze shoulder blades
- Control the lowering phase

**Concentration Curls:**
- Sit, elbow braced against inner thigh
- Curl slowly, squeeze at top
- Great for bicep peak development

### Workout C: Shoulders & Arms (Pump Day)

#### Warm-Up (5 minutes)
- Arm circles (1 minute)
- Wall slides (1 minute)
- Light lateral raises with 3kg

#### Main Workout

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Weight | Rest |
|----------|------|------|--------|------|
| **Shoulder Press** (standing) | 4 | 10-12 | 8kg | 60s |
| **Lateral Raises** | 4 | 15 | 5kg | 45s |
| **Front Raises** (alternating) | 3 | 12 each | 5kg | 45s |
| **Bent-Over Reverse Flyes** | 3 | 15 | 5kg | 45s |
| **Arnold Press** | 3 | 10-12 | 8kg | 60s |
| **Superset: Curls + Kickbacks** | 3 | 12 each | 5kg | 60s |
| **21s Bicep Curls** | 2 | 21 | 5kg | 90s |
| **Tricep Dips** (chair/bench) | 3 | 15-20 | Bodyweight | 60s |

#### Exercise Notes

**Arnold Press:**
- Start with palms facing you at shoulder height
- Rotate palms outward as you press up
- Hits all three deltoid heads

**21s Bicep Curls:**
- 7 reps bottom half only
- 7 reps top half only
- 7 reps full range of motion
- Incredible pump!

**Lateral Raises Tips:**
- Lead with elbows, not hands
- Slight bend in elbows
- Control the negative (lowering)

## Progressive Overload Strategy

Since you have limited weight options, use these methods to progress:

### Week 1-4: Foundation
- Focus on form and mind-muscle connection
- Use moderate weights (5kg for most exercises)
- Complete all prescribed reps with control

### Week 5-8: Volume Increase
- Add 1 set to compound movements
- Move to 8kg for main exercises
- Reduce rest periods by 15 seconds

### Week 9-12: Intensity Techniques
- Add drop sets (8kg → 5kg → 3kg)
- Include pause reps (2 second pause at bottom)
- Slow negatives (4 seconds lowering)
- Supersets for arm exercises

### Beyond 12 Weeks
- Consider heavier dumbbells (see equipment recommendations)
- Add resistance bands for variety
- Increase push-up difficulty with weighted vest or backpack

## Equipment Recommendations

### Essential Additions (Priority Order)

#### 1. Resistance Bands Set - ~$25-40
**Why:** Adds variable resistance, excellent for:
- Band-assisted stretching
- Face pulls (shoulder health)
- Banded push-ups
- Curls and tricep work with different resistance curves

**Recommended:** Set with multiple resistance levels (light to heavy)

#### 2. Pull-Up Bar (Doorframe) - ~$25-35
**Why:** Game-changer for upper body development:
- Pull-ups and chin-ups (best lat and bicep builder)
- Hanging for shoulder decompression
- Leg raises for core

**Recommended:** Multi-grip doorframe bar

#### 3. Adjustable Dumbbells OR Heavier Set - ~$100-200
**Why:** Your 8kg will become too light within 2-3 months
- Options: 12kg and 16kg fixed dumbbells
- OR adjustable dumbbells (more versatile, space-saving)

**Recommended:** Adjustable set going up to 20-25kg

#### 4. Dip Station/Parallettes - ~$40-60
**Why:** Enables:
- Proper dips (chest and tricep focus)
- L-sits
- Elevated push-up variations
- Bodyweight rows

#### 5. Foam Roller - ~$15-25
**Why:** Essential for:
- Mobility work
- Recovery between sessions
- Thoracic spine extension

## Tips for Arm Growth

### Training Principles
1. **Volume is key** - Arms recover quickly; train them 2-3x per week
2. **Full range of motion** - Don't cheat the curls
3. **Mind-muscle connection** - Focus on squeezing the target muscle
4. **Slow negatives** - 3-4 second lowering builds more muscle
5. **Don't neglect triceps** - They're 2/3 of your arm size!

### Nutrition Essentials
- **Protein:** 1.6-2.2g per kg bodyweight daily
- **Calories:** Slight surplus needed for growth
- **Post-workout:** Protein + carbs within 2 hours
- **Hydration:** Extra important with cardio training

### Recovery
- Sleep 7-9 hours (growth happens during sleep)
- Don't train sore muscles (alternate properly)
- Cardio after weights, not before (if same day)
